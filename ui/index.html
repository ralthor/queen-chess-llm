Queen's reasoning

Make a move or press Analyze position. Queen writes its analysis in its own token notation; every move it mentions becomes a chip here. Hover a chip to see that moment of the line on the board, click to step into it.

How to read Queen's notation

Queen doesn't write Nf6. It describes the board with 77 special tokens, always from the point of view of the side to move (“me”):

<PIECE_M?> my piece, <PIECE_O?> opponent's piece; ? is P N B R Q K.
<SQUARE_n> square n = 1…64, counted from my bottom-left corner (a1 for White, a8 for Black). Turn on Square numbers to see them on the board.
A move is piece · from · [captured piece] · to · [promotion]. Castling is the king moving two squares.
